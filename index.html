How to read your results

Latency

Lower is generally better. This is round-trip time to the selected host, not necessarily your game's server.

Jitter

Average change between consecutive replies. Lower and steadier is generally better.

Packet loss

Missing replies can indicate congestion or filtering. Some hosts treat ping differently.

Before changing settings

Try Ethernet

A wired connection often reduces Wi-Fi interference and variability.

Pause heavy downloads

Downloads and uploads can increase latency when your connection is busy.

Restart your router

Useful for some temporary issues, but not a guaranteed ping fix.

Avoid random registry tweaks

Unverified tweaks can break settings and rarely offer a reliable gaming advantage.
